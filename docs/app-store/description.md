## Description

**AgentHub is the App Store for AI agents.**

Browse a curated marketplace of AI-powered agents, each designed for a specific task. No prompt engineering required — just fill in a form and tap Run.

**WHAT YOU CAN DO**

- Write professional emails, thank-you notes, and complaint letters
- Explain confusing bills and legal documents in plain English
- Generate meal plans and grocery lists from what's in your fridge
- Prepare for appointments with organized talking points
- Build your daily schedule with AI-powered time blocking
- Set up automations that run on a schedule and notify you with results

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Browse the Agent Store and install agents you need
2. Open an agent, fill in the inputs, tap Run
3. Get a focused AI response in seconds
4. Optionally, set up automations to run agents on a schedule

**AUTOMATIONS (Pro & Unlimited)**

Set it and forget it. Schedule agents to run at specific times and receive the results as a push notification:
- Daily Plan Builder — Get your prioritized schedule every morning at 7am
- Evening Reset — Recap your day and prep for tomorrow at 9:30pm
- Weekly Money Check-in — Review spending every Friday at 5pm

**BRING YOUR OWN KEY**

Already have an OpenAI or Anthropic API key? Add it to AgentHub and get unlimited AI calls on any tier — even Free. Your keys are encrypted and never stored in plaintext.

**SUBSCRIPTION TIERS**

- **Free** — 3 agents, 10 AI calls/day, BYOK supported
- **Starter ($9.99/mo)** — 15 agents, 75 AI calls/day
- **Pro ($29.99/mo)** — 50 agents, smart + deep AI, 3 automations
- **Unlimited ($99.99/mo)** — Unlimited everything, 10 automations

New users get a **7-day free trial** of Pro.

**PRIVACY FIRST**

- No ads, no tracking, no data selling
- Your data is encrypted and isolated — only you can see it
- BYOK keys encrypted with AES-256-GCM
- AI outputs are never used to train models
